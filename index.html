Investments

SPOT pools investments and finds the structure best suited to the nature of each project. To us, investment means more than capital: know-how, sales contacts and networks can be contributed just as well. Depending on the project, we structure financing as equity, mezzanine or debt. Our focus: companies, real estate and situations – we look for the special opportunity.

Advisory

In selected projects, we also contribute our experience as investors, entrepreneurs and owners – including the rights and responsibilities that come with an equity position. We know the challenges of shareholder and partnership situations both first-hand and from the numerous projects we have had the privilege to accompany. Where it makes sense, we support entrepreneurs as advisors in structuring such situations, without seeing ourselves as a consulting firm at our core.

Ecosystem

We see ourselves as part of an ecosystem of investors, entrepreneurs, managers and experts that has grown over many years. SPOT has been an integral part of this ecosystem since 2005 – for more than two decades – and, together with our partners, has realized numerous projects in a wide variety of constellations.

Ask us

Our work is based on personal relationships. If you have received our card or a message from us, feel free to ask us about our projects – or whether a project of yours, as an investment or in an advisory capacity, might be one for us. You will receive a prompt and honest answer.
